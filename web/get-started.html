Increment

A free workout log built around progressive overload. Log your sets, see what you lifted last time, and watch your numbers go up.

Free · No account · No ads · Works offline

Add it to your home screen

Increment runs in your browser, but it's best on your home screen: it opens like an app, works without a signal and keeps your data safe.

iPhone and iPad
  1. In Safari, tap Share (the square with the arrow; in newer Safari it's under ⋯).
  2. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add. If you see Open as Web App, leave it on.
  3. Open Increment from your home screen from now on.

Chrome on iPhone works the same way: Share → Add to Home Screen. Safari can delete a website's data after seven days without a visit; the home screen app keeps it.

Android
  1. In Chrome, tap Install Increment above, or ⋮ (top right) → Add to home screen → Install.
  2. Open Increment from your home screen or app drawer.

Samsung Internet and Firefox have Add to home screen in their menus too.

Computer
  1. In Chrome or Edge, click Install Increment above, or the install icon at the right end of the address bar.
  2. In Safari on a Mac: File → Add to Dock.

Or simply use it in a browser tab.

Your data

Everything you log stays on your device. There's no account and no server, so nobody else has a copy. Every so often, and before you switch phones, tap the avatar on Today → Back up now.

Coming from Strong? In Strong, Settings → Export Workouts. Then in Increment, the avatar on Today → Import from Strong.
