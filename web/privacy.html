Privacy policy

Increment, the workout log for iPhone, Android and the web (increment-training.app). Last updated 7 November 2026.

In short: Increment collects nothing about you. There's no account, no server, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. What you enter stays on your device, and the developer never sees it.

Who is responsible

Laurin Libossek

What the app stores, and where

Everything you enter: workouts, routines, exercises, notes, body weight and measurements, your settings, and the name you can enter under Account. The phone apps save it in their own storage on your phone, the web app in your browser's storage on your device. Neither sends any of it anywhere; there's no server it could go to.

Body weight and measurements are health data. They stay on your device like everything else.

If your phone's own backup is on (iCloud Backup on iPhone, Google backup on Android), it can include the app's data. Apple or Google then store it under their privacy policies.

To delete everything, use Account → Delete all data, or delete the app. For the web app, deleting the website's data in your browser does the same.

Internet

The app doesn't send anything over the internet and works fully offline. The web app is loaded from the internet (see "The web app" below), but what you enter stays on your device there too.

What you send yourself

  • Backups and CSV exports are only made when you tap Back up now or Export as CSV. On the phone the share sheet opens, and you choose where the file goes (Files, iCloud Drive, Google Drive, AirDrop, email); the web app downloads the file. From then on, the place or service you chose handles it.
  • Shared routines: Share on a routine makes a link with that routine's name, notes, exercises and sets, nothing else. You choose who gets it. The routine is in the part of the link after "#", which browsers don't send to any server. Whoever opens the link in a browser loads the web app (see "The web app" below).
  • Imports: the app only reads files you pick yourself: a CSV export from another workout app, or a backup to restore.

Permissions

  • Notifications, only in the phone apps and only for the rest timer: when a rest is over and the app isn't open, a notification arrives. The phone schedules it itself; no push service is involved. The app asks the first time a rest timer starts. You can turn it off under Account → Rest timer alerts, or in your phone's settings. The web app doesn't send notifications.
  • The app doesn't use the camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos, Apple Health or Health Connect.

The web app

The web app at increment-training.app is the same app in the browser, and this page is on the same website. Both are hosted on GitHub Pages. When your browser loads them, GitHub receives your IP address and the usual request data, like any website does (GitHub's privacy statement). The website sets no cookies and loads nothing from anyone else.

Once opened, your browser keeps a copy of the app on your device, so it also opens without a connection. With a connection, the browser asks GitHub whether there's a new version.

If you write to the developer

Emails to are forwarded by Cloudflare to the developer's Gmail inbox, so Cloudflare and Google process them under their privacy policies (Cloudflare, Google). Your email is only used to answer you. You can also open an issue on GitHub; issues there are public.

Apple and Google

You get the phone apps from the App Store or Google Play. Apple and Google process data about that, such as your account and the download, under their own privacy policies. If you've allowed it in your phone's settings (on iPhone: Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements → Share With App Developers), they show the developer anonymous statistics such as the number of installs and crash reports. These don't include your workouts or anything else you entered in the app.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct and delete your data, to restrict its use, to take it with you, and to object, and you can complain to a data protection authority. The developer has no data about you, apart from emails you send, so there's nothing else on the developer's side to hand over or delete. Your data is on your device, where you can export it (Back up now, Export as CSV) and delete it (Delete all data) at any time.
